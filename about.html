Who We Are

YSPH-JNFLS is a student public health society at Jinan Foreign Language School, focusing on tuberculosis prevention, health communication, research practice, and community action.

Through reading, discussion and public health communication, we bring reliable knowledge into everyday life.

We are also a TB Prevention Hut certified by the Chinese Anti-Tuberculosis Association under the China Association for Science and Technology, helping spread accurate, clear, and humane TB knowledge in schools and communities.

01 / TB Prevention Hut

Our Mission: make health knowledge understandable.

From understanding disease to reducing stigma, we support school and community health education with clear explanations.

Step One

Understand TB

Use short text, visuals, and Q&A to explain what tuberculosis is.

Step Two

Learn prevention

Explain transmission, screening, ventilation, and treatment adherence in everyday language.

Step Three

Reduce fear and stigma

Make protection more scientific and support more humane.

Step Four

Carry knowledge beyond campus

Share public health knowledge through volunteer teaching, service, and community partnerships.

02 / Our Activities

Our Activities

Bringing public health education to people and communities in remote mountain areas.

In mountain-area classrooms, we share public health knowledge with children through conversation and accessible lessons.

From TB literacy to everyday health, we listen, learn, and make knowledge more relevant to daily life.

Audience Remote mountain communities
Method Classes, materials, dialogue
Focus Understandable health knowledge
Direction From literacy to action
