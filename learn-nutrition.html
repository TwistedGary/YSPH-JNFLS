Balanced Nutrition

Make sense of everyday food choices through variety.

Sources checked: 8 September 2026 · YSPH educational overview

On this page

01 / EXPLAINED

Beyond a single food

Healthy eating concerns an overall pattern: adequacy, balance, moderation and diversity. A single food cannot replace a varied diet.

02 / EXPLAINED

What variety means

Include vegetables, fruit, whole grains, pulses and suitable protein sources. Choices depend on age, activity, cultural preferences and available foods.

03 / EXPLAINED

Pay attention to sugar and salt

Frequently choosing foods high in free sugars, sodium and unhealthy fats can undermine dietary quality. Compare nutrition labels on similar products rather than relying on front-of-pack claims.

COMMON MISCONCEPTION

Can a superfood make a diet healthy?

No. Adequacy, variety and moderation across the diet matter more than a special label on one food.

One thing to put into practice

Look at the food groups in one meal and consider adding variety.
