TB EDU HUB

TB Edu Hub: the International Curriculum Centre is listed in the first cohort

Notice No. 82 (2026) from the Chinese Antituberculosis Association lists the International Curriculum Centre of Jinan Foreign Language School in the first cohort of its youth TB education hut initiative. The notice sets requirements for construction, team training and reporting. TB Edu Hub focuses on sharing TB knowledge with school and community audiences.
